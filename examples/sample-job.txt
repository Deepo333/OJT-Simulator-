Product Designer — Growth
Northwind Analytics · Remote (US) · Full-time

About the role
Northwind Analytics is looking for a Product Designer to join our Growth
squad. You will work alongside product managers, engineers, and a small
research team to design and ship experiments across our onboarding, activation,
and pricing surfaces. Our customers are data-driven teams at mid-market
SaaS companies, and our product is the analytics workspace they open first
thing in the morning.

What you'll do
- Own end-to-end design for one or more Growth workstreams (onboarding,
  activation, upgrade, or referrals) — from problem framing through
  shipped experiment.
- Design and prototype quickly in Figma; run weekly usability sessions on
  UserTesting or Maze and turn the transcripts into concrete design
  changes.
- Partner with our PM and engineers to instrument every experiment in
  Amplitude and write the analysis doc after each ship.
- Contribute components back to our design system (Figma library + a
  small React codebase); we expect you to raise the bar for consistency
  across surfaces.
- Present work weekly at design critique and monthly at the company-wide
  product review.

What we're looking for
Required:
- 4+ years of product design experience, at least 2 of which shipping
  consumer-grade B2B SaaS.
- A portfolio that shows shipped work, not just concept explorations.
  We care most about the "before / after" and the reasoning between them.
- Strong Figma fluency, including auto-layout and component APIs.
- Comfort partnering with data — reading Amplitude funnels, forming
  hypotheses, and writing crisp experiment analyses.
- Excellent written communication. Most of our team is async and
  distributed across four time zones.

Preferred:
- Prior experience on a Growth or Activation team.
- Front-end skills (HTML, CSS, light React) enough to prototype in code
  or file precise engineering tickets.
- Experience running or scoping quantitative research (surveys,
  card-sorts, MaxDiff).
- Familiarity with our stack: Amplitude, Segment, Statsig, Notion,
  Linear.

What we offer
Competitive salary, equity, remote-first culture with quarterly team
onsites, generous learning budget, and top-tier medical/dental/vision.

Northwind is an equal-opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and
are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.
